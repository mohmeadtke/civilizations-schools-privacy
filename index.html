Overview

This application (مدرسة الحضارات) provides students with access to school information, lessons, study materials, announcements, and notifications. Students can sign in to view personalized content and receive important updates. It is developed and published by محمد تقي علي. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Data Collection

We collect and store only the essential information necessary for app functionality:

  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the app to improve our services

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial information, health records, or other private details. All data is stored securely on our backend servers and is used solely for providing app functionality.

Device Permissions

The app may request the following permissions to provide its services:

  • Internet Access: Required to connect to our servers and fetch school information, lessons, and updates
  • Notifications: To send you important school announcements and updates
  • Storage: To cache content for offline viewing and improve app performance

We do not access sensitive device permissions such as location, contacts, microphone, or camera unless specifically required for app functionality.

The information we collect is used to:

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your password is encrypted and stored securely. We regularly review our security practices to ensure your information remains protected.

Third-Party Services

Data Sharing
