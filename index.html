Privacy Policy

مدرسة الحضارات

Developer: محمد تقي علي

Effective Date: October 15, 2025

Overview

This application (مدرسة الحضارات) provides students with access to school information, lessons, study materials, announcements, and notifications. User accounts are created by authorized school personnel and provided to students. Students simply sign in with provided credentials to access educational content. It is developed and published by محمد تقي علي. We are committed to protecting user privacy and maintaining data security.

Data Collection

We do NOT collect personal data from app users.

Here's how our system works:

  • Account Creation: User accounts are created exclusively by authorized school personnel, not by users themselves
  • Credentials: Usernames and passwords are generated and provided by school staff to students
  • No Personal Information: Users do not input personal information during app usage
  • Authentication Only: The app only verifies provided credentials to grant access to educational content

While the app may request access to certain device features (camera, photos, or location) for functionality, no data is collected, stored, or shared by the developer.

Device Permissions

The app requests minimal permissions necessary for core educational features:

  • Internet Access: Required to connect to our servers and display educational content
  • Notifications: To receive important school announcements (optional - can be disabled in device settings)
  • Camera: Used only to allow students to take photos of homework assignments and upload them securely
  • Photo Library: Used to select and upload homework files or choose a profile picture
  • Location (When In Use): Used only to help track the school bus in real time for student safety

We do NOT request access to:

  • Microphone
  • Contacts or call logs
  • SMS or messaging
  • Device identifiers beyond what's necessary for app functionality

Any permissions granted are used solely for educational purposes and not for data collection, storage, or sharing.

How We Use Your Data

Since we do not collect personal data from users, this section explains how the app functions:

  • Authentication: Verify credentials provided by school personnel to grant access
  • Content Delivery: Display educational materials, lessons, and school information
  • Homework Uploads: Allow students to take or upload homework photos through the camera or photo library
  • Bus Tracking: Display school bus location using GPS for safety
  • Notifications: Deliver school announcements and updates (if enabled)
  • App Functionality: Ensure smooth operation and user experience

No user behavior tracking, analytics, or personal data processing occurs within this application.

Data Security

Since no personal data is collected from users, security concerns are minimal. However, we maintain:

  • Secure Authentication: Login credentials are transmitted securely
  • Content Protection: Educational materials and uploaded homework are served through secure connections
  • No Personal Data Storage: No personal user information is stored on our servers
  • Privacy by Design: The app is designed to function without collecting personal data

Third-Party Services

We do NOT use any third-party services that collect user data. Specifically:

  • No analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.)
  • No advertising networks or SDKs
  • No user tracking or behavioral analysis tools
  • No social media integrations that share data
  • No crash reporting services that collect personal information

The app operates independently without external data collection services.

Data Sharing

We do NOT share any user data because we do not collect personal data.

Since no personal information is collected or stored:

  • There is no personal data to share with third parties
  • No user information is sold or traded
  • No behavioral data is shared for advertising purposes
  • Account credentials remain within the school's internal system

Children's Privacy

This app is fully compliant with children's privacy regulations including COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act):

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect any personal information from users of any age
  • Safe for All Ages: The app can be safely used by children without privacy concerns
  • Educational Purpose: Content is purely educational and appropriate for school environments
  • Limited Device Permissions: Camera, photo, and location access are only used for learning-related features such as homework uploads and bus tracking

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, traditional data protection rights are not applicable. However:

  • Access Control: You can control what content you view within the app
  • Notification Settings: You can disable notifications in your device settings
  • Account Management: Contact school personnel for any account-related requests
  • App Removal: You can uninstall the app at any time without data concerns

For any account-related inquiries, please contact the school administration or use the contact information below.
